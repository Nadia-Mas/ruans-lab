01 / Mission

We bridge artificial intelligence and biology to uncover patterns that matter—from molecules and cells to health and disease.

Our lab creates rigorous computational methods, open tools, and collaborative research that make complex biological data more interpretable and actionable.

Interpretable Machine Learning
