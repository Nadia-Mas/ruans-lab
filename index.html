About the Lab

Building computational methods for complex biological questions

Our research brings together machine learning, data science, genomics, and computational biology to better understand biological systems and improve biomedical discovery.

We are interested in developing accurate, interpretable, and responsible computational approaches that can contribute to scientific discovery and precision health.
